The problem

Selling for bitcoin on Nostr is still fragile

Every storefront wants its own copy of your shop, payments are hard to prove, and relays forget. Infinite Markets fixes the plumbing.

01

Fragmented catalogs

The web shop and each marketplace keep their own copy; stock drifts and you oversell.

One authoritative catalog, published as open Nostr events.

02

"Was it paid?"

Nostr receipts and buyer-stated amounts aren't proof; retries create duplicate invoices.

Only settled LNbits payments confirm orders.

03

Browser-bound shops

Orders wait until the merchant's tab or signer is online.

Background workers inside LNbits run 24/7.

04

Unreliable relays

Publishing isn't delivery, deleted items linger, and nothing records who accepted what.

Durable outbox with per-relay evidence and a live catalog check.

05

Leaky, legacy protocols

NIP-15 is unrecommended and NIP-04 messages reveal who buys from whom.

NIP-99 + Gamma spec, with NIP-17 gift-wrapped orders.

06

Platform lock-in

Marketplaces can end up holding the funds and owning the customer relationship.

You keep the key, the stock, the wallet and the customers.

How it works

Two rails, one pipeline

Infinite Markets is the commerce authority: catalog, inventory, shipping, orders and messages. LNbits core stays the settlement authority: invoices, payment records and your funding source.

Rail 1 · web

A storefront you own

  • Categories, collections, filters and sorting
  • Lightning checkout with private order links
  • Sign in with Nostr or an email magic link
  • Theme presets, 4 layouts, dark mode, embeds
Rail 2 · Nostr

Native Nostr commerce

  • NIP-99 listings, collections and shipping options
  • Encrypted NIP-17 orders to your own inbox relays
  • NIP-89 handler: clients deep-link to your pages
  • Merchant profile with NIP-05 and Lightning address
Shared pipeline

Every order, same path

  1. Validate and reprice from current data
  2. Reserve stock atomically (15-minute hold)
  3. LNbits creates an invoice bound to the order
  4. Settlement confirms; expiry releases stock
No duplicate invoices. No double allocation. Relay delivery is never mistaken for payment truth.
Publish everywhere

Marketplaces become storefronts, not landlords

Your catalog is published as standard Gamma / NIP-99 events signed with your own key. Buyers check out in the marketplace they already use; the order arrives encrypted in your inbox and goes through the same reservation and invoice pipeline as a web order.

  • InventoryOne stock count across every channel, held in your LNbits
  • ShippingYour zones and rules, published for marketplaces to quote
  • PaymentsInvoices from your wallet, on your funding source; no custody
  • CustomersEncrypted threads and email in your own admin
  • IdentityYour Nostr key, portable across every Nostr app

Built on LNbits

The wallet platform underneath

LNbits is a free, open-source Lightning wallet and accounts system, in development since 2018. Infinite Markets only ever asks it to create invoices on your wallet; it never holds spending keys.

Any funding source

LND, Core Lightning, Phoenixd, Alby, Strike, Blink, NWC, Breez, Spark and more. Switch without touching balances.

Isolated wallets

Admin keys spend; invoice keys only receive. Apps get just what they need.

Extension platform

Real Python apps with their own database, routes, UI and always-on background tasks.

Merchant Stack

TPoS, Inventory, Orders, WebShop, plus NostrMarket (NIP-15, legacy), Nostr Relay and Nostr Client. Infinite Markets is the NIP-99 next generation. Next: Inventory and Shipping integration roadmap

Status & risks

Honest about where it stands

Where it stands

  • Web commerce, Nostr orders, buyer accounts and catalog import/export implemented
  • v0.5 published via an LNbits extension manifest (sha256-verified)
  • Conformance suite plus Plebeian tests and a live listing check

Risks

  1. Experimental software: v0.5 on a draft spec; start small
  2. Needs the community: interop depends on marketplaces, the spec group, and real merchants
  3. Maintenance costs: tracking LNbits, spec and client changes takes ongoing time

What's next

  • Seeking testers and active users: run it, send feedback, file issues
  • Live plebeian.market checkout; Conduit and Shopstr testing
  • Structured addresses for marketplace physical orders
  • LNbits Inventory and Shipping integration
Get started

Install in three steps

You need LNbits 1.6.0 or newer and a wallet to receive payments. Try it with small amounts first.

Full install guide →

Add the manifest

In LNbits: Server → Extensions → Manifest sources.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitkarrot/infinitemarkets/main/manifest.json

Set four host variables

INFINITEMARKETS_MASTER_KEYS, …_ACTIVE_KEY_VERSION, …_PRIVACY_KEY and …_PUBLIC_BASE_URL. The extension won't start without them.

Bind a wallet and publish

Pick the merchant wallet, add a product, and it appears on your storefront and on Nostr.
