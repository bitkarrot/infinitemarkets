## Phase Goal

**As a** buyer, **I want** a sign-in link emailed to me that lands me in my order history — including orders I placed before I had an account — **so that** losing a private link never loses my purchases; **and** once inside, **I want** to prove and attach my other identity (email ↔ Nostr key) so the same history follows either credential.

(Story source: Phase 03.1 CONTEXT D-05..D-11.)

<objective>
Ship the entire server-side magic-link and identity-linking vertical: the `channel='account'` email transport that renders AEAD'd links at send time, the no-oracle request/verify endpoints with dual rate limits and the §5.4 fragment landing page, verified-email order binding counts, and the prove-both link flows ending in a representability-checked union merge — plus the spec amendment and a dedicated runtime suite.

Purpose: D-05..D-11 are one mechanism family (hash-only single-use tokens → verified identity → account). Landing them together keeps token custody, rate posture, and merge semantics consistent.

Output: `enqueue_magic_link` + worker branches, `/nostr/email/*` + `/nostr/link/*` routes, `GET /auth/email` + fragment JS, union-merge service, `test_email_signin.py`, spec amendment.
</objective>
